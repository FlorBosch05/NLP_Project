# Test and benchmark status

The version 3 dataset audit checked 30 structured recipes and 20 reference cases for the specific conditions recorded in `results/data_audit_v3.json`. It reported no structural or selected-reference errors. That is not independent or culinary validation: see `DATA_REVIEW_v3.md` for the unverified times, substitutions, ingredient labels and other limitations.

`results/retrieval_comparison_20261125_132837.json` contains the 13-question section-versus-whole-recipe retrieval experiment described in `RETRIEVAL_RESULTS_v3.md`. It is a local labelled-evidence proxy, not a Ragas or answer-quality benchmark.

The 20-question V3 run `results/evaluation_sections_k5_20261125_133312.json` was blocked by the `openai/gpt-oss-20b` daily token quota; its 20 errors are not model-quality results. Later attempts at Q11–Q13, `results/evaluation_sections_k5_20261125_133919.json` and `results/evaluation_sections_k5_20261125_134229.json`, stopped at Q11 for the same reason, even after the initially indicated retry time. After quota recovery, retry the missing questions using `evaluate.py` and `--offset`; see the README. Do not report these rate-limit errors as failed recipe answers.

The separately saved 120B-judge batch `results/ragas_batch_20261125_133044_197900.json` scores saved **V2** answers from `v2/results/evaluation_sections_k5_20261125_130014.json`. All three metrics were produced for all 13 retrieval cases. The matching summary `results/ragas_summary_20261125_134248_298500.json` reports mean context precision 0.798, context recall 1.000 and faithfulness 0.855. These are LLM-judge estimates on a small, synthetic and not independently reviewed set, not guarantees of real-world accuracy. Q03 faithfulness was approximately 0.444, consistent with the previously observed unsupported detail. This batch does not validate the V3 answers or corpus. Do not combine its results with 20B-judge scores.

The later Qwen V3 experiment generated answers and completed Ragas scoring for the 13 retrieval cases; see `QWEN_EVALUATION_v1.md`. Those results apply to Qwen-generated answers judged by Qwen, not the default GPT-OSS-20B application. They do not compare the whole-recipe strategy. Section chunking therefore remains a provisional choice based on the small local retrieval comparison, not a demonstrated Ragas winner. The corpus and references still require independent human review.
